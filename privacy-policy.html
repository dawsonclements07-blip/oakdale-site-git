Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Oakdale Exterior Cleaning ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect when you contact us or request a quote, and how we use it.

What We Collect

When you submit a quote request or fill out a form on our website or through our Facebook/Instagram ads, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address (if provided)
  • Details about the service you're interested in

How We Use It

We use this information only to:

  • Contact you about your quote request
  • Schedule and confirm appointments
  • Follow up about services you've asked about

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Text Messages & Calls

By submitting your phone number, you agree that Oakdale Exterior Cleaning may contact you by phone or text message regarding your quote request. Message and data rates may apply. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time.

Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party tools to operate our business, including form processing (Formspree), scheduling (Cal.com), and advertising platforms (Meta/Facebook). These providers may process your information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Retention

We keep quote request information only as long as needed to respond to your request and provide services, or as required by law.

Your Choices

You can request that we delete your information or stop contacting you at any time by reaching out using the contact information below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
